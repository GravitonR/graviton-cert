\begin{abstract}
Consciousness science after the COGITATE adversarial collaboration (\emph{Nature}, 2025) faces a twin impasse: classical theories disagree on how temporal integration binds conscious perception while accounting for both anesthesia and generalized seizures, and quantum-brain proposals routinely violate 310~K decoherence walls, ignore correlated cortical noise, or rely on unbracketed pharmacological and isotope confounds. Here we present \textbf{Relational Temporal Binding Theory (RTBT v2.0)}, formally verified in Lean~4 (\code{graviton-cert v1.0.0}, Studies~12--13: \code{NQA-01}--\code{NQA-07} and \code{RTBT-01}--\code{RTBT-12}, bringing the full suite to \textbf{100/100 zero-dependency proofs} with zero \code{sorryAx} and zero custom axioms). RTBT postulates that conscious access arises inside a two-sided Goldilocks band where a balanced excitatory--inhibitory (E/I) thalamocortical assembly maintains an internally constructed, thermodynamically dissipative \emph{relational clock} whose \emph{differentiated} temporal binding capacity $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{diff}} = (1-\rho)\,R_\phi^2\,(\mathrm{SNR}_1^2/\rho)/(1-(w_E-w_I))$ exceeds $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{crit}}$ over a nested hierarchy of finite optimal windows. Beyond micro-to-macro obstructions (No-Free-Coupling, Correlated-Noise Ceiling $\mathrm{SNR}_{\mathrm{pop}}^2 < \mathrm{SNR}_1^2/\rho$, and Bracketed $^{128}\mathrm{Xe}/^{129}\mathrm{Xe}/^{130}\mathrm{Xe}$ Cancellation), RTBT~v2.0 formally closes all five canonical referee objections: (1)~\emph{Seizure / Hypersynchrony Collapse} proving $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{diff}} < \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{crit}}$ as $\rho \to 1^-$ even at $R_\phi^2 = 1$; (2)~\emph{Wilson--Cowan E/I Derivation} of recurrent gain, GABAergic anesthesia suppression, and epileptic disinhibition blow-up; (3)~\emph{Nested Multi-Timescale Sensory Hierarchies} and cubic optimality for asymmetric $1/\tau^2 + B\tau$ colored noise; (4)~\emph{Macroscopic Synaptic ATP Power Floors} tightening thermodynamic collapse from $k_B$ to the real 20~W cerebral scale; and (5)~\emph{Exact Empirical Falsification Criteria} paired with a 4-regime clinical classifier and explicit Hard Problem quarantine (\code{RTBT-GATE-01}).
\end{abstract}

\end{enumerate}
