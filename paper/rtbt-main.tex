\begin{abstract}
The COGITATE adversarial collaboration (\emph{Nature}, 2025) revealed structural vulnerabilities in both Integrated Information Theory and Global Neuronal Workspace Theory, while quantum-brain proposals remain constrained by 310~K thermal decoherence, shared synaptic noise correlations, and pharmacological confounds. Here we develop \textbf{Relational Temporal Binding Theory (RTBT)}, a mathematically formalized, falsifiable framework verified in Lean~4 (\code{graviton-cert}, Studies~12--13: 36 zero-dependency theorems, \code{NQA-01}--\code{07} and \code{RTBT-01}--\code{29}, zero \code{sorryAx}, zero custom axioms) and tested via continuous-time stochastic differential equation (SDE) simulations. RTBT postulates that unified conscious access arises inside an algebraically connected ($\lambda_2(L) > 0$), closed-loop ($w_{AB}w_{BA} > 0$), delay-coherent ($4d < v\tau_{\mathrm{win}}$), balanced excitatory--inhibitory (E/I) cortical assembly whose finite-$N$ differentiated temporal binding capacity $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{exact}} = (1-\rho)R_\phi^2 \frac{N\,\mathrm{SNR}_1^2}{1+(N-1)\rho}\frac{1}{1-(w_E-w_I)}$ and covariance participation ratio $D_{\mathrm{eff}}(N,\rho) = \frac{N}{1+(N-1)\rho^2}$ exceed critical thresholds over a nested hierarchy of optimal finite integration windows. We prove continuous-time Lyapunov stability and critical slowing down, exact Fisher information and Cram\'er--Rao estimation ceilings, two-sided collapse in both anesthesia and generalized seizures, exclusion of parallel cerebellar architectures and low-dimensional or disconnected oscillators, Kelz--Proekt bistable anesthesia hysteresis, Sperry split-brain callosotomy bifurcation, macroscopic 20~W synaptic ATP power floors, Bell-singlet quantum no-signaling across neurons, and a zero-coherence $\mu$s-to-100~ms chemical spin-modulation bridge. Continuous-time SDE simulations confirm that perturbational Lempel--Ziv complexity peaks in the awake band and collapses under both propofol and seizures, while the metaphysical Hard Problem (\code{RTBT-GATE-01}) and wet-lab calibrations (\code{RTBT-GATE-02}--\code{03}) remain explicitly open.
\end{abstract}
