\begin{abstract}
The COGITATE adversarial collaboration (\emph{Nature}, 2025) revealed structural vulnerabilities in both Integrated Information Theory and Global Neuronal Workspace Theory, while quantum-brain proposals remain constrained by 310~K thermal decoherence, shared synaptic noise correlations, and pharmacological confounds. Here we develop \textbf{Relational Temporal Binding Theory (RTBT)}, a mathematically formalized, falsifiable framework verified in Lean~4 (\code{graviton-cert}, Studies~12--13: 36 zero-dependency theorems, \code{NQA-01}--\code{07} and \code{RTBT-01}--\code{29}, zero \code{sorryAx}, zero custom axioms), tested via continuous-time stochastic differential equation (SDE) simulations, and evaluated empirically across \textbf{72 consecutive human subjects (210 EEG epochs)} from five open-access PhysioNet and OpenNeuro clinical cohorts. RTBT postulates that unified conscious access arises inside an algebraically connected ($\lambda_2(L) > 0$), closed-loop ($w_{AB}w_{BA} > 0$), delay-coherent ($4d < v\tau_{\mathrm{win}}$), balanced excitatory--inhibitory (E/I) cortical assembly whose finite-$N$ differentiated temporal binding capacity $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{exact}} = (1-\rho)R_\phi^2 \frac{N\,\mathrm{SNR}_1^2}{1+(N-1)\rho}\frac{1}{1-(w_E-w_I)}$ and covariance participation ratio $D_{\mathrm{eff}}(N,\rho) = \frac{N}{1+(N-1)\rho^2}$ exceed critical thresholds over a nested hierarchy of optimal finite integration windows. Across real human clinical EEG (\code{tools/rtbt-eeg-analyzer.py}), RTBT's biomarkers separate Wake from deep N3 sleep ($N=20$, $\tau_{1/e}$ AUROC $0.914$, $p=3.81\times 10^{-6}$), separate covert REM dreaming from N3 sleep directly corroborating \code{RTBT-14} ($\widehat{\mathcal{B}}$ AUROC $0.946$, $19/20$ patients, $p=3.81\times 10^{-6}$), achieve $20/20$ ($100\%$) eyes-closed resting specificity, detect pre-LOC vs.\ pre-ROC spectral entropy neural inertia in surgical general anesthesia ($N=7$, $6/7$ patients, $p=0.0469$), and document explicit real-world failure modes (sleep-onset seizures, focal discharges, and frontal ocular blink confounds), while the metaphysical Hard Problem (\code{RTBT-GATE-01}) and wet-lab calibrations (\code{RTBT-GATE-02}--\code{03}) remain explicitly open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The 2025 COGITATE adversarial collaboration~\cite{COGITATE2025} shifted consciousness science toward pre-registered falsifiable predictions after observing neither sustained posterior synchronization (as strict IIT required) nor robust stimulus-offset prefrontal ignition (as strict GNWT required). In parallel, Working Paper~05~\cite{WP05} (\code{BQ-01}--\code{06}, DOI~\texttt{10.5281/zenodo.24577879}) formalized three single-neuron biophysical gates separating spin-protected microdomain chemistry from 310~K charge decoherence and classical pharmacological confounds.

Yet single-neuron exclusion gates do not specify how macro-scale cortical networks bind experience across time, nor why consciousness vanishes under surgical anesthesia, generalized epileptic seizures, and cerebral hypoxia, yet persists during REM dreaming and complete locked-in syndrome, bifurcates after callosotomy, and is absent in the 69-billion-neuron cerebellum, stateless feedforward networks, or low-dimensional physical oscillators. \textbf{Relational Temporal Binding Theory (RTBT)} unifies these constraints within a single machine-checked dynamical and information-theoretic framework.

\section{Dynamical Postulate, Lyapunov Stability \& Fisher Information (\code{RTBT-01}--\code{29})}

\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{exact}} = (1-\rho)\,R_\phi^2\,\frac{N\,\mathrm{SNR}_1^2}{1+(N-1)\rho}\,\frac{1}{1 - w_{\mathrm{eff}}} > \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{crit}},
  \qquad
  D_{\mathrm{eff}}(N,\rho) = \frac{N}{1 + (N-1)\rho^2} \ge D_{\min}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Continuous-Time Lyapunov Stability, Critical Slowing \& Fisher--Cram\'er--Rao Floor; \code{RTBT-28}, \code{29}]

\section{Multi-Cohort Empirical Evaluation on Public Human Clinical EEG (\texorpdfstring{$N=72$}{N=72})}

Every recording undergoes identical, deterministic preprocessing: $1$--$45\,\mathrm{Hz}$ cosine-tapered FFT bandpass filtering, automatic bad-channel rejection ($>6\times$ median channel SD or flatline), montage-aware Common Average Referencing (CAR) on dense monopolar caps ($\ge 8$ channels), and computation of exact covariance participation ratio $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ (\code{RTBT-19}), normalized graph Laplacian Fiedler connectivity $\lambda_{2,\mathrm{norm}}$ (\code{RTBT-23}), channel-median autocorrelation decay time $\tau_{1/e}$ (\code{RTBT-28}), Welch spectral entropy $H_{\mathrm{spec}}$, and Schartner--Kaspar--Schuster Hilbert-envelope \code{LZ76} complexity~\cite{Schartner2015} (\code{RTBT-22}).

\textbf{Principal Empirical Findings \& Honest Failure-Mode Audit.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Strong Validation of Critical Slowing (\code{RTBT-28}) and Covert REM Binding (\code{RTBT-14}):} In $N=20$ consecutive overnight hospital PSG patients (\code{hmc-sleep-staging}, $0$ skipped), deep slow-wave sleep (N3) nearly tripled $\tau_{1/e}$ ($20.6 \to 60.4\,\mathrm{ms}$, $19/20$ patients, $d_z=-2.16$, $p=3.81\times 10^{-6}$, AUROC $0.914$) while depressing \code{LZ76} ($0.861 \to 0.687$, $p=1.91\times 10^{-5}$, AUROC $0.899$). Crucially, during covert REM dreaming, RTBT's binding index rebounded sharply above N3 in $19/20$ patients ($0.121$ vs.\ $0.056$, $p=3.81\times 10^{-6}$, AUROC $0.946$), corroborating \code{RTBT-14} on real hospital recordings. Across all 55 PhysioNet subjects ($95$ differentiated vs.\ $35$ collapsed epochs), pooled \code{LZ76} achieved AUROC $0.893$ ($95\%$ CI $0.835$--$0.944$).
  \item \textbf{Neural Inertia Asymmetry (\code{RTBT-25}) \& Propofol Dreaming Bifurcation:} In OpenNeuro \code{ds004541} ($N=7$ complete surgical general anesthesia trajectories with documented \code{start}, \code{LOC}, and \code{ROC}), spectral entropy $H_{\mathrm{spec}}$ was significantly lower immediately before emergence (\code{pre-ROC}) than immediately before loss of consciousness (\code{pre-LOC}) ($6/7$ patients, $d_z=+1.04$, $p=0.0469$), accompanied by longer $\tau_{1/e}$ ($38.9$ vs.\ $31.2\,\mathrm{ms}$, $6/7$ patients). In Storm Lab's repeated-awakening propofol dataset (\code{ds005620}~\cite{Bajwa2024}, $N=10$), light sedation produced paradoxical GABAergic beta speedup ($\tau_{1/e}$ $20.7 \to 19.5\,\mathrm{ms}$, $p=0.0215$), while deeper sedation (\code{task-sed2}) bifurcated cleanly between volunteers preserving covert-dreaming complexity (\code{LZ76} $\sim 0.82$--$0.89$) and those crossing into deep slow-wave suppression (\code{sub-1016}: $\tau_{1/e}$ $19.1 \to 92.5\,\mathrm{ms}$, \code{LZ76} $0.850 \to 0.697$; \code{sub-1036}: $\tau_{1/e}$ $21.8 \to 62.7\,\mathrm{ms}$).
  \item \textbf{Explicit Failure Modes Disclosed Openly (\code{AUDITS/rtbt-*-validation.md}):} First, while real-time sliding-window tracking on \code{chb01\_03} captured seizure onset ($2996\,\mathrm{s}$) and post-ictal slow-wave offset ($3036\,\mathrm{s}$) to the second ($D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ $7.00 \to 4.63$, \code{LZ76} $0.730 \to 0.518$) and $D_{\mathrm{eff}}$ dropped in $12/15$ ($80\%$) CHB-MIT patients, unconditioned \code{LZ76} dropped in only $9/15$ ($p=0.454$) because overnight pediatric seizures (\code{chb06}, \code{chb10}, \code{chb14}) often begin out of deep N3 sleep (baseline \code{LZ76} $0.46$--$0.50$), where ictal scalp EMG ($>20\,\mathrm{Hz}$) or focal discharges inflate raw \code{LZ76}. Second, while Eyes-Closed resting specificity reached $20/20$ ($100\%$), $4/20$ Eyes-Open EEGMMIDB recordings exhibited frontal blink delta ($61$--$73\%$) despite awake-level \code{LZ76} ($0.71$--$0.84$). Third, raw surgical OR recordings (\code{ds004541}) contained $3{,}500$--$18{,}400\,\mu\mathrm{V}$ railed electrodes requiring automated bad-channel rejection before CAR. These audits define exact preprocessing requirements for prospective clinical trials (\code{paper/preregistration-brief.md}).
